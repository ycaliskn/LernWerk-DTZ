# Content roadmap

Content is released in level-specific packs. Each row is a planned content group, not a claim that the current prototype already includes the complete group.

## A1 – release pack 1

**Vocabulary topics (planned order; grouped from Goethe’s official A1 inventory)**
1. Person: names, addresses, contact details, family, personal relationships, nationality, appearance and daily routines.
2. Wohnen: rooms, furniture, household items, rental arrangements and moving.
3. Umwelt: plants, animals, climate and weather.
4. Reisen/Verkehr: private and public transport, travel, accommodation and luggage.
5. Essen/Trinken: food, meals, dishes, drinks, restaurants and cafés.
6. Einkaufen/Gebrauchsartikel: shops, prices, payment, groceries and clothing.
7. Dienstleistungen: post, telecommunications, banks and police; health-service vocabulary is included as a practical daily-life supplement.
8. Erziehung/Ausbildung/Lernen: childcare, school and language learning.
9. Arbeit/Beruf: workplace and holiday.
10. Freizeit/Unterhaltung: interests, sport, radio/TV, internet and reading/press.

**Verb groups (candidate inventory; verify against the official list before publishing)**
- Core irregular verbs: sein, haben, werden, gehen, kommen, fahren, sehen, sprechen, essen, lesen, schlafen.
- Regular everyday verbs: machen, lernen, wohnen, arbeiten, kaufen, fragen, antworten, spielen, hören, sagen, suchen, brauchen.
- Modal and polite forms: können, müssen, wollen, möchten, dürfen (introduce only where confirmed by the chosen A1 syllabus).
- For each verb: infinitive, Präsens all persons, Präteritum where pedagogically useful, Perfekt, auxiliary verb, separability/reflexivity, example sentence and audio.

**Grammar topics (planned editorial sequence, not a verbatim official Goethe grammar list)**
1. Personal pronouns and verb position in main clauses.
2. Präsens regular and common irregular conjugation; sein / haben.
3. Articles and grammatical gender: der / die / das; ein / eine.
4. Singular/plural and noun spelling.
5. W-questions and yes/no questions.
6. Negation: nicht / kein.
7. Nominativ and introductory Akkusativ.
8. Possessive articles: mein / dein / sein / ihr / unser.
9. Separable verbs and word order.
10. Modal verbs in everyday sentences.
11. Time expressions and common prepositions.
12. Basic imperative and polite requests.

## A2 – release pack 2

Add longer descriptions, plans, experiences, comparisons, everyday problem solving, travel, appointments, services, health, education, housing, workplace and written messages. Add Perfekt mastery, Präteritum of frequent verbs, Dativ, two-way prepositions, subordinate clauses with weil/dass/wenn, comparatives, reflexive verbs, modal verbs and sentence connectors after validating the Goethe A2 list and DTZ scope.

## B1 – release pack 3

Add opinions, reasons, advantages/disadvantages, experiences, work and applications, media, environment, society, education, health, travel and formal/informal communication. Add subordinate clauses, relative clauses, passive, Konjunktiv II, adjective endings, verb-preposition combinations, nominalised expressions and connectors. Map practice sets to DTZ task types.

## Quality gates for each pack

- Source register checked and dated.
- Each noun has article, plural and an example in context.
- Each verb has a validated conjugation set and Perfekt auxiliary.
- Each grammar rule has a short explanation, positive/negative examples and at least three distractors reviewed by a second person.
- Translation reviewed by a fluent speaker for each language pack.
- Image rights/source and audio voice rights documented.
- Unit tests for answer keys and progression; manual QA on iOS and Android.
