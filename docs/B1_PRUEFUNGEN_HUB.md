# B1 Prüfungen — separate exam hub

The B1 Prüfungen entry is separate from vocabulary, verbs and grammar so exam simulations do not contaminate learning progress.

The hub has four independent modules:

- **Hören** — audio tasks, answer sheet, replay rules and transcript/review after completion.
- **Lesen** — matching, notices, public information, text comprehension and letter gap-fill tasks.
- **Schreiben** — prompt, recipient/purpose checklist, structured writing space and a reviewed sample response.
- **Sprechen** — picture description, personal questions and gemeinsam etwas planen dialogues.

The first preview intentionally leaves the task sets empty. New exam questions will be independently authored; official model sets are used to check format and level, not copied as a question bank.

Each module will have its own progress, attempt history and review archive. A mock exam should store its own attempt result without changing vocabulary/grammar completion counts.
