# LernWerk A1–B1 grammar curriculum and references

Checked: 25 November 2026

## Source policy

There is no single Goethe-Institut document that should be presented as a complete, official DTZ grammar syllabus for A1, A2 and B1. LernWerk therefore uses a layered source method:

1. **BAMF DTZ handbook, section 8.4 “Strukturen”** is the exam-specific structural reference for DTZ at A2–B1. It identifies grammatical structures that candidates should understand and use, and explains that grammar is a resource for communicative action rather than an end in itself. Section 9 contains the DTZ word lists. The handbook's first edition is 2009 and it contains a copyright notice; this project links to it and uses it for scope checks, not as a source to reproduce its protected text or test items.
2. **BAMF Rahmencurriculum für Integrationskurse**, especially section E 2.7 “Grammatik lernen”, informs the learning method: noticing, organising and practising structures as part of learning German for everyday tasks.
3. **DVV / vhs-Lernportal “Themen und Grammatik” A1, A2 and B1 PDFs** provide open, level-specific course outlines. They are the main source for the staged grammar sequence below.
4. **DW Nicos Weg A1/A2 grammar overviews** are secondary open references for cross-checking A1/A2 topic placement.
5. **Goethe-Institut A1/A2/B1 exam-preparation pages** provide official sample tasks and level checks. Goethe word lists are used to verify vocabulary scope; they are not described as a complete grammar catalogue.

The grammar explanations, original examples, distractors and quizzes shipped in LernWerk must be written independently. Do not copy complete paragraphs, exercise items, images or protected course contents from a reference source. Keep source attribution and licence notes with every content release.

## Level sequence

### A1 — Basic sentences and everyday communication

Main reference: DVV / vhs-Lernportal, *Themen und Grammatik – A1-Deutschkurs* (2019), 2-page overview; cross-check: DW Nicos Weg A1 and Goethe A1 sample tasks.

- Personal pronouns and present-tense conjugation (Präsens).
- Main-clause word order; verb in second position; W-questions and yes/no questions.
- Formal and informal address (Sie / du).
- Definite, indefinite and negative articles; noun gender and plural.
- Possessive determiners.
- Basic accusative and dative patterns; common local prepositions.
- Negation with *nicht* and *kein*; time expressions and inversion.
- Common irregular and separable verbs; basic modal verbs.
- Imperative; *haben* and *sein* in Präteritum; introductory Perfekt with *haben/sein*.
- Basic comparison, demonstratives and ordinal numbers.

### A2 — More detailed expression and linked sentences

Main reference: DVV / vhs-Lernportal, *Themen und Grammatik – A2-Deutschkurs* (2019), 2-page overview; cross-check: DW Nicos Weg A2, Goethe A2 sample tasks and the DTZ structural inventory.

- Accusative/dative personal pronouns and indefinite pronouns.
- Two-way prepositions: location (dative) versus direction (accusative).
- Comparison and basic adjective declension.
- Perfekt with mixed, separable and inseparable verbs.
- Reflexive verbs and verb-preposition combinations.
- Subordinate clauses with *weil, dass, wenn*; connectors such as *aber, denn, deshalb, trotzdem*.
- Temporal prepositions and sequencing adverbs.
- Modal verbs in present and past forms.
- Konjunktiv II for polite requests and suggestions.
- Indirect W-questions, relative clauses and present passive at introductory level.
- Common dative/accusative verb complements and possessive determiner forms.

### B1 — Independent narration, argumentation and complex structures

Main reference: DVV / vhs-Lernportal, *Themen und Grammatik – B1-Deutschkurs* (2019), 2-page overview; cross-check: BAMF DTZ handbook section 8.4 and Goethe B1 sample tasks.

- Adjective declension in nominative, accusative and dative; genitive.
- Paired conjunctions: *entweder … oder, zwar … aber, sowohl … als auch, weder … noch*.
- Subordinate clauses with *obwohl, da, falls, indem, bevor, während, seitdem, nachdem*.
- Purpose and infinitive clauses: *um … zu, ohne … zu, (an)statt … zu, damit*.
- *Infinitiv mit zu*, verb-preposition combinations and prepositional relative clauses.
- Konjunktiv II for suggestions, requests, wishes and unreal conditions.
- Future; passive present with modal verbs and passive past.
- Präteritum (regular, irregular, mixed and modal verbs); Plusquamperfekt.
- Indirect yes/no questions; *als* versus *wenn* in past narratives.
- Past participles as adjectives; *je … desto/umso*; prepositional adverbs.
- Word formation with prefixes/suffixes and indefinite pronouns.

This is a learning sequence, not a claim that every listed construction is tested in every DTZ exam. In the app, topic explanations should be tied to everyday situations and communicative tasks.

## App attribution requirements

- The dedicated **“Resmî kaynaklar”** page lists the source organisation, level, document title, direct link, purpose and rights note.
- Each A1/A2/B1 grammar block lists the references supporting that level.
- A grammar exercise screen includes a shortcut to its references.
- When new grammar items are added, add source attribution to `app/sources.js` and update this document in the same change.

## Implemented A1 demo grammar pack (v0.2)

The current A1 pack contains 20 short grammar exercises aligned to the VHS-Lernportal A1 overview (12 lessons). It covers personal pronouns and *sein*, statement/question word order, possessive articles, indefinite/negative articles, predicative adjectives, plural/zero article, accusative, separable verbs, time expressions/inversion, dative prepositions, imperative, modal verbs, Perfekt, comparative forms, demonstratives and ordinal dates. This is a curated practice pack, not a claim that every A1 grammar point or every Goethe exam demand is exhausted.
